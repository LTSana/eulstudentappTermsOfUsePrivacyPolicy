## Purpose of the App

The EUL Student app is designed to provide a comprehensive, modern, and unified platform for students of the European University of Lefke (*Lefke Avrupa Üniversitesi*). The primary objectives of the App are as follows:

1. **Dual-System Academic Integration (OIBS & SIS)**: The App enables students to conveniently access and organize their official academic records, including cumulative GPA (CGPA), semester grades, 4-year degree curriculum progress, enrolled courses, and weekly lecture timetable blocks.
   
2. **Moodle LMS Integration & QR Attendance**: The App allows students to access their Moodle accounts, utilize 1-tap course enrollment, and scan classroom QR codes with the device camera to log lecture attendance directly on Moodle.

3. **Weekly Schedules & Notification Radar**: Students receive intelligent push notifications for upcoming lectures, classroom changes, early morning digests, and campus transit departures.

4. **Tuition & Accounting Overview**: Students can monitor snapshot balances across multiple currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, TRY) and view tuition transaction records for convenient personal tracking.

5. **Campus Transit Explorer & Live Maps**: The App provides edge-to-edge interactive vector transit maps, real-time departure countdowns, stop itineraries, and walking directions for campus shuttle routes.

6. **Privacy & Discreet Tools**: The App includes on-device sensor gestures ("Flip to Hide") and Live Activities / Dynamic Island widgets to enhance student privacy and daily campus convenience.

## Data Collection and Usage

### Purpose of Data Collection

The EUL Student app is designed to assist students from the European University of Lefke in managing their academic life securely. To achieve this objective, the App handles the following data:

1. **Student Portal & Moodle Credentials**: Used solely to authenticate with university servers (OIBS, SIS, Moodle LMS, and Portal). Credentials are stored encrypted in the device hardware keychain (Apple Keychain / Android Keystore) and are never shared externally.

2. **Academic & Timetable Records**: Ingested upon your consent to construct your unified schedule, course transcript, and degree matrix. Records are cached locally on your device in SQLite for offline access.

3. **Camera Access (QR Attendance)**: Used in real time exclusively to scan classroom attendance QR codes and submit verification to Moodle. No photos, videos, or camera frames are recorded, saved, or uploaded.

4. **Device Sensors (Accelerometer)**: Processed locally in memory to detect when the device is flipped face-down for the "Flip to Hide" feature, concealing grades and accounting balances.

5. **Location Data (Maps & Transit)**: GPS coordinates are processed strictly on-device to calculate distances to nearby bus stops and walking directions. Live GPS locations are never stored on our servers, transmitted to third parties, or logged.

### Third-Party Subprocessors & Infrastructure

We integrate with trusted industry-standard infrastructure partners:

- **Supabase**: Cloud database synchronization protected with Row Level Security (RLS).
- **RevenueCat**: In-app purchase receipt verification with Apple App Store and Google Play.
- **Sentry**: Anonymized crash logging, bug diagnostics, and performance monitoring.
- **Expo Notifications**: Delivery of opt-in push notifications for lectures and shuttle reminders.
- **OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap**: Open-source vector map tiles and routing.

### App Tracking Transparency & Telemetry

- The App **does not track users across third-party apps or websites** for advertising purposes.
- Telemetry collected via Sentry is strictly used for anonymized crash analysis and app stability.

### Data Retention & Account Deletion

In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and global privacy standards:
- You retain complete ownership of your data.
- You can clear your local SQLite cache at any time.
- You can permanently delete your entire student profile, stored preferences, and notification tokens by selecting **Delete Account & Data** within the App's **Settings > University Account**.

### Language Support and Translation Accuracy

The App supports multiple languages (English, Turkish, Russian, Portuguese). Translations are provided for convenience; students should verify official academic notices directly on university source portals.
