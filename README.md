### 2. Purpose of the Application
The Application provides a unified student utility, academic assistant, and campus & town navigation platform for students, faculty, staff, local residents, and visitors of the European University of Lefke (*Lefke Avrupa Üniversitesi*) in Lefke and Gemikonağı, Northern Cyprus. 

Primary features include:
1. **Dual-System Academic Integration (OIBS & SIS):** Secure client-side synchronization of cumulative grade point averages (CGPA), semester transcripts, 4-year Bologna curricula requirements, enrolled course lists, and interactive weekly timetables.
2. **Moodle LMS Integration & QR Attendance:** Mobile web service session acceleration, course overview synchronization, one-tap course enrollment, and camera-based QR attendance verification.
3. **Campus Transit & Moving Shuttle Tracker:** Edge-to-edge interactive vector transit maps (powered by OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap), real-time departure countdowns, stop itineraries, and walking directions.
4. **Multi-Currency Tuition Ledger:** Offline snapshots of university accounting ledger balances across multiple global currencies (TRY, USD, EUR, GBP) and verified payment transactions.
5. **AI Academic Practice Quizzes:** On-demand multiple-choice drill sessions and flashcard explanations generated from student-selected syllabus topics, lecture notes, or uploaded slides.
6. **Community Discovery & Storefront Directory:** Interactive digital directory of local campus and town cafes, restaurants, bakeries, markets, and pharmacies with digital menu browsing, amenity tags (Wi-Fi, power outlets), and plain-text order slip generation.
7. **Native 3D LiDAR & Architectural Scanning:** RoomPlan and ARKit spatial mesh scanning for indoor campus navigation and student architectural reference.
8. **Digital Student Card & Mifare NFC Pass:** Gyroscope-reactive virtual student identification card, physical Mifare Classic 1K NFC card pairing, and turnstile barcode generation.

# Part II: Privacy Policy & Data Protection Declaration

### 1. Overview & Data Philosophy
Your privacy is fundamental to our software design. We operate on a strict principle of **data minimization**:
- We collect only the data strictly necessary to provide core academic, transit, and campus services.
- We **never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data** to advertising brokers or marketing third parties.
- All high-security academic records, credentials, and biometric gates are processed on-device whenever possible.

### 2. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

#### A. University Portal Credentials
To fetch your class schedules, semester grades, Moodle courses, and tuition balances, the Application requires your student portal credentials (OIBS, SIS, Moodle, or Portal).
- **On-Device Keychain:** All credentials entered on your device are saved into hardware-encrypted secure storage (Apple Keychain on iOS / Android Keystore on Android).
- **Zero Plaintext Cloud Storage:** When background ingestion services are utilized, credentials stored in the backend vault are sealed using **AES-256-GCM** authenticated envelope encryption (`v1:<nonce>:<ciphertext+tag>`). The plaintext password cannot be accessed without runtime cryptographic keys.
- **Session Lifespans:** Portal sessions (`JSESSIONID`, `ASP.NET_SessionId`, `MoodleSession`) are held in ephemeral memory and discarded upon session completion.

#### B. Academic Records & Tuition Data
- Cumulative GPA (CGPA), earned credits, letter grades, Bologna curriculum matrices, and weekly timetables are synchronized via secure TLS connections and cached locally in an on-device SQLite database for instant offline access.
- Multi-currency snapshot balances and historical payment records are retained strictly for personal student reference and budgeting.

#### C. Location & Campus Transit Data
- Transit calculations and campus shuttle stops utilize open-source vector tiles (OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap).
- Your physical GPS location is queried **strictly on-device** via `expo-location` to determine distances to shuttle stops and display pedestrian walking directions.
- **Your live GPS coordinates are never transmitted to our backend servers, stored in databases, or logged.**

### 3. Hardware Sensors, Camera, LiDAR & Biometrics

- **Camera:** Accessed exclusively with explicit permission to scan classroom QR codes for Moodle attendance verification. No video feeds or photographs are ever uploaded, recorded, or retained without deliberate student action.
- **Apple ARKit & LiDAR Spatial Scanners:** On supported iOS devices equipped with LiDAR, the camera and spatial depth sensors are used via Apple ARKit and RoomPlan to generate 3D architectural floor plans. **All generated 3D room meshes and USDZ exports are stored 100% locally on your device** and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.
- **Device Biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint):** Biometric authentication gates (such as the app cold-start lock screen and CGPA privacy unmasking) are executed **strictly within the device's hardware Secure Enclave** via the operating system's local authentication framework. The Application never reads, receives, or stores raw biometric data.
- **Near-Field Communication (NFC):** Used solely to read the physical serial identifier (UID) of your Mifare Classic 1K university smart card to pair your digital turnstile pass.
- **Motion Sensors (Accelerometer):** Monitored locally to power the "Flip to Hide" feature, which temporarily masks academic grades when the device is inverted.

### 4. Artificial Intelligence & Study Material Processing
The AI Practice Quiz Hub integrates the Google Gemini API (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite`) to generate educational drills:
- **Transient Data Transmission:** When you request an AI quiz, your selected course topic, syllabus text, or uploaded document slides are transmitted securely via TLS directly to Google Cloud enterprise endpoints solely for real-time question generation.
- **No Foundation Model Training:** In accordance with Google Cloud enterprise terms, **your uploaded study notes, documents, and student prompts are never used to train, retrain, or improve public artificial intelligence models**.
- Quiz history, questions, and scores are stored locally in the student's on-device SQLite database.

### 5. Community Discovery & Order Delivery Privacy
- Discovery venues, menus, reviews, and community amenities are public records stored in Supabase with authenticated Row-Level Security (RLS).
- **Delivery Addresses:** Saved delivery addresses are encrypted and accessible only to your authenticated account ID.
- **Plain-Text Order Slips:** When placing a food order, the Application formats a plain-text order slip containing only your selected items, contact phone, and delivery address. This slip is dispatched directly via WhatsApp or your chosen communication channel. **No credit card numbers, payment card tokens, or banking details are collected, processed, or stored by the Application.**
